# World Seed Architecture Overview

**Public abstraction:** intentionally omits private contracts, schemas, algorithms, and strategic implementation detail.

World Seed is organized as a connected ecosystem rather than a single game or application.

## Major domains

### Emergent world simulation
A Simulation Kernel coordinates authoritative state, events, consequences, replay, and provenance. World laws define intended relationships such as intent, exchange, resonance, thresholds, progression, transformation, balance, and emergence.

### Playable world
The first vertical-slice direction centers on one Seed, one Familiar, and one Chamber, later opening into areas such as the Battledome. Local implementations may advance a playable slice behind stable interfaces while deeper systems remain unfinished.

### Intelligent companion architecture
The project explores memory, possibility, epistemics, intent, consent, relationship, and cadence systems for an AI companion. Predictions and inferred patterns are not treated as the user's thoughts, identity, consent, or authorization.

### Embodied and wearable computing
World Seed extends through a watch experience, Android companion, sensor fusion, a personal signal protocol, a sovereign body bus, and a compute mesh. Development is staged from ordinary devices toward more advanced hardware research, with safety and consent enforced independently.

### Research, creative, and economic branches
The architecture is intended to support research, creator tools, a store/economic layer, and other future branches without collapsing them into one undifferentiated system.

## Governance across domains

The private master preserves stable identity, reality-versus-belief separation, event history, provenance, explicit unknowns, explainability, consent boundaries, and clear status labels. Public material is selected from that larger record; it does not supersede it.
