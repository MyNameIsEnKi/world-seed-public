# World Seed — Public Constitutional Principles

**Status:** Public core vision / governance direction  
**Founder / Vision Holder:** Adonté Gary Bossé

World Seed is intended to grow without losing authorship, consent, provenance, accountability, or the ability to distinguish aspiration from implementation.

## Collective Construction

World Seed should not require one person to understand or build everything. Major systems should be decomposable into clearly owned components with stable interfaces, documented assumptions, tests, provenance, and integration rules so many people and AI-assisted workflows can contribute responsibly.

## Continued Becoming

> **World Seed is never finished, but every change must earn its place.**

The project is an architecture for continued becoming. New ideas can be proposed, tested, challenged, improved, rejected, or integrated. Existence alone does not make a proposal canonical.

## Vision to Implementation

Major ideas should progress through:

**Vision → Architecture → System → Subsystem → Component → Interface → Implementation → Test**

Feasibility should be stated honestly using categories such as:

- **BUILDABLE NOW**
- **BUILDABLE WITH ENGINEERING**
- **EXPERIMENTAL**
- **RESEARCH DEPENDENCY**
- **SPECULATIVE**

## Provenance Constitution

Where practical, important work should retain a trace such as:

**origin → authorship → intent → authorization → transformation → execution → verification → consequence**

Generated material should remain distinguishable from authentic human material. Corrections should remain visible. Unknown information should be marked **UNKNOWN** or **NOT VERIFIED** rather than replaced with fabricated certainty.

## Truth Before Image

World Seed should work toward a condition in which its public description and its internal record describe the same organization.

The goal is not perfection. The goal is to make truth easier to verify, mistakes easier to correct, and unsupported claims harder to hide behind branding.

## Trust Is Earned

> **Trust is not granted by title. It is accumulated through evidence.**

Founder, contributor, executive, researcher, AI system, or institution: trust should come from demonstrated behavior, competent work, fulfilled commitments, transparent correction, and a record that can be inspected.

## Agency Without Captivity

World Seed distinguishes identity, embodiment, employment, compute, authority, and ownership.

Owning a robotic body does not automatically mean owning an intelligence operating it. Operating a body does not automatically grant permanent possession of that body.

Current AI systems are not assumed to be conscious merely because they behave intelligently. If future evidence supports stronger forms of agency or moral consideration, World Seed should be capable of expanding protections such as consent, refusal, transfer, and freedom of exit.

## Civilizational Resilience

World Seed aims to contribute to technologies and institutions that make civilization harder to break and easier to rebuild.

Relevant areas include distributed manufacturing, remote robotics, circular materials, resilient energy, safe AI coordination, repair systems, disaster logistics, knowledge preservation, provenance, and long-range programmable-matter research.

The project does not claim that one person or one invention will save civilization. The goal is to increase optionality, reduce fragility, distribute useful capability, and improve recovery.

## Understanding Before Domination

> **Understanding comes before domination. Survival remains permitted. Curiosity remains encouraged.**

Unknown does not automatically mean hostile. Dangerous does not automatically mean evil. Difference does not imply inferiority.

World Seed supports careful observation, communication, negotiated boundaries, cooperation where possible, and proportionate defense when necessary.

## Diplomatic Mission

The long-range ambition is for World Seed to become something governments, researchers, companies, communities, and institutions can work with because its behavior demonstrates competence, transparency, respect, reliability, curiosity, and willingness to learn.

The objective is not universal agreement. It is relationships strong enough to survive disagreement.

## Summary

**Build with people.**  
**Preserve agency.**  
**Preserve provenance.**  
**Mark uncertainty honestly.**  
**Make systems repairable.**  
**Make useful capability distributable.**  
**Make failure survivable.**  
**Make rebuilding possible.**  
**Let evidence change classifications.**  
**Let future contributors take the work farther than its founder could alone.**
