# World Seed — First Contact & Diplomatic Principles

**Status:** Public philosophy and operating direction

World Seed treats the unknown as something to investigate before judging where circumstances permit.

The standing principle is:

> **Preserve life. Establish facts. Communicate intent. Reduce misunderstanding. Defend if necessary. Learn regardless of outcome.**

## Contact sequence

A high-level first-contact sequence is:

**Detect → Observe → Interpret → Communicate → Establish Boundaries → Cooperate Where Possible → Defend If Necessary → Learn**

## Threat classification

World Seed keeps these categories separate:

- **UNKNOWN** — insufficiently understood.
- **BENIGN** — no meaningful threat observed.
- **COOPERATIVE** — reciprocal interaction and coordination established.
- **HAZARDOUS** — capable of causing harm regardless of intent.
- **DEFENSIVE** — protecting itself, territory, offspring, resources, or another legitimate interest.
- **HOSTILE** — intentionally causing serious harm or imposing unacceptable control.
- **IMMEDIATE THREAT** — imminent danger requiring rapid protective action.

Appearance alone does not determine category.

## Communication before escalation

Where practical, teams should first attempt to establish shared recognition, then a minimal common language, then boundaries and reciprocal expectations.

Contact without boundaries is not diplomacy.

## Defense

Understanding does not require helplessness. Defensive force may be used to protect life and prevent serious harm, and should be proportionate to the threat where circumstances reasonably allow.

The purpose of defense is to end danger, not punish the unknown.

## Special Envoy concept

World Seed's long-range lore and governance architecture includes a Special Envoy role for situations where normal diplomatic channels fail or a contact requires unusually broad interdisciplinary judgment.

For Adonté Gary Bossé, the ideal ambassadorial role is based on trust rather than command: being someone who can enter situations where trust does not yet exist, listen to specialists, communicate across cultures and disciplines, and help create a path toward cooperation.

## Real-world boundary

There is currently no confirmed public evidence of extraterrestrial civilizations requiring this protocol. The public principles are therefore a combination of game-world architecture, diplomacy design, and a general framework that could also apply to unfamiliar future AI, robotics, scientific, or cross-cultural situations.
