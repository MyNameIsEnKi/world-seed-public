# World Seed — Public Showcase

> A curated public record of an emergent simulation ecosystem, AI companion architecture, embodied-computing platform, resilience mission, and creative world.

**Founded, originally conceived, and directed by Adonté Gary Bossé, publishing as [MyNameIsEnKi](https://github.com/MyNameIsEnKi).**

World Seed connects game architecture, persistent simulation, autonomous characters, knowledge and possibility systems, wearable computing, robotics concepts, diplomacy, creative tools, and responsible human–AI collaboration. Its long-range goal is an ecosystem whose worlds, companions, devices, research branches, and institutions can grow together without losing authorship, consent, provenance, or human accountability.

This repository is the deliberately limited public window into a larger private project. It establishes provenance, explains the architecture at a safe level, and records selected milestones. It is **not** the private Design Bible or a complete source mirror.

## Core vision

World Seed is meant to be built collaboratively. No single person should need to understand or construct the entire system alone. Major ideas should be decomposable into clearly owned components with stable interfaces, documented assumptions, provenance, tests, and integration rules so specialists, creators, community members, and AI systems can contribute without losing the larger vision.

World Seed is also intended as an architecture for continued becoming:

> **World Seed is never finished, but every change must earn its place.**

The public constitutional principles extend this into provenance, trust, resilience, agency, diplomacy, and contact doctrine.

## Public principles now recorded

World Seed publicly commits to developing around ideas including:

- collaborative construction rather than dependence on one person;
- vision-to-implementation decomposition;
- honest feasibility categories;
- provenance and attribution;
- trust accumulated through evidence rather than title;
- separation of identity, embodiment, employment, compute, authority, and ownership;
- civilizational resilience and distributed capability;
- remote embodiment and long-range programmable-matter research;
- understanding before domination;
- diplomacy, communication, and proportionate defense when necessary;
- explicit boundaries between verified reality, proposal, fiction, and speculation.

## Claim discipline

World Seed separates **canonical design**, **proposal**, **implemented**, **verified**, **historical**, and **speculative** status. A document describing a system does not claim that the system has been built or tested. Public records are intentionally conservative about implementation and evidence.
