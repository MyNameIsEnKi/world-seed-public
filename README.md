# World Seed — Public Showcase

> A curated public record of an emergent simulation ecosystem, AI companion architecture, embodied-computing platform, and creative world.

**Founded, originally conceived, and directed by Adonté Gary Bossé, publishing as [MyNameIsEnKi](https://github.com/MyNameIsEnKi).**

World Seed connects game architecture, persistent simulation, autonomous characters, knowledge and possibility systems, wearable computing, creative tools, and responsible human–AI collaboration. Its long-range goal is an ecosystem whose worlds, companions, devices, and research branches can grow together without losing authorship, consent, provenance, or human authority.

This repository is the deliberately limited public window into a larger private project. It establishes provenance, explains the architecture at a safe level, and records selected milestones. It is **not** the private Design Bible or a complete source mirror.

## Core vision

World Seed is meant to be built collaboratively. No single person should need to understand or construct the entire system alone. Major ideas should be decomposable into clearly owned components with stable interfaces, documented assumptions, provenance, tests, and integration rules so specialists, creators, community members, and AI systems can contribute without losing the larger vision.

The full public statement is in [CORE_VISION.md](CORE_VISION.md), including the **Collective Construction Rule**, the vision-to-implementation path, feasibility classifications, and the principle that future development teams should be able to give concrete answers about whether a proposed World Seed capability can actually be built.

## Claim discipline

World Seed separates **canonical design**, **proposal**, **implemented**, **verified**, and **historical** status. A document describing a system does not claim that the system has been built or tested. Public records are intentionally conservative about implementation.
