# Contributing to World Seed

World Seed is owner-directed. Public visibility is an invitation to follow selected progress, not permission to redefine the project or treat suggestions as canon.

Substantive contributions are currently by invitation or prior agreement. Ideas may be proposed through a clearly labeled issue. Unsolicited code or large content submissions may be closed without use.

For invited work:

1. Use a separate branch.
2. Keep the pull request focused.
3. Label it as proposal, implementation, test, documentation, or correction.
4. State what was tested and what remains unverified.
5. Include no secrets, personal data, private World Seed material, or unlicensed third-party material.
6. Wait for owner review before treating it as accepted or canonical.

Contributors remain credited for their accepted work. Contribution does not transfer ownership unless a separate written agreement says so. See [RIGHTS.md](RIGHTS.md).
